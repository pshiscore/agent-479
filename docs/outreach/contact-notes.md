# Other outreach: feed requests

These sources can't be read automatically today, either because they block
non-browser requests or because their robots.txt disallows the feed format.
Each needs only a short request for a feed link. Send them whenever
convenient; none of them block Phase 1.

## City of Springdale
- **Contact:** Vicki Satterfield, Information Specialist, City Administration,
  vsatterfield@springdalear.gov, (479) 750-8110 (from a web search; confirm on
  <https://www.springdalear.gov/page/contact-and-connect/>)
- **Why:** springdalear.gov shows a JavaScript bot challenge to automated
  requests, and we don't get around those.
- **Ask:** "Is there an iCal/ICS or RSS feed for the city's community events
  (not public meetings) that a local events calendar may subscribe to?"

## Downtown Springdale Alliance
- **Contact:** use the contact form or email on
  <https://www.downtownspringdale.org/> (no address found on the events page)
- **Why:** their Squarespace robots.txt disallows the `?format=ical` and
  `?format=json` feeds.
- **Ask:** permission to subscribe to their events iCal feed, or a preferred
  way to receive event listings.

## Bentonville Public Library
- **Contact:** library@bentonvillear.com (on bentonvillelibrary.org)
- **Why:** the site returns 403 to automated requests.
- **Ask:** "Do you have an iCal/ICS or RSS feed for public events that a
  local events calendar may subscribe to?"

## Short template

> Hi, I'm [Your name], a local resident building Agent 479, a free events
> calendar for Northwest Arkansas. I'd like to include [organization]'s public
> events, listing just the title, date, time, place, and a link back to your
> page. Do you have an iCal/ICS or RSS feed we could subscribe to? If you'd
> rather we not list your events, just say so. Thanks!
> [Your name]
