# Invite email: organizers who post events only on social media

**Why:** many organizers (fire departments, festivals, markets, churches,
clubs) post only on social media, which we never fetch
(`docs/product/organizers.md`, Tier 3). This invites them to either post on
a calendar we can read or use the "Submit an event" form. Send it after a
benchmark miss points to an organizer (a "no source" registry task), or when
you meet one in person.
**To:** the organizer's own contact address, found in your browser.
**From:** your personal email, with **Reply-To: bot@479.guide**.
**Fill in before sending:** `[Name]`, `[Organization]`, `[Event they ran]`,
`[Your name]`. Delete the paragraph that doesn't fit (website or no website).
**Before the site is public:** the form sits behind the preview login until
launch, so hold this email until then, or offer the email route only.

---

**Subject:** Listing [Organization]'s events on 479 Guide (free)

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your name], and I run **479 Guide** (<https://479.guide>), a free
calendar of public events in Benton and Washington counties.

I saw [Event they ran] and would like to list events like it. We list facts
only (what, when, where) with a link back to your own page, and we check each
one before it appears. There's no cost, and we don't copy your descriptions or
photos.

**If you have a website:** a calendar on it with an iCal or RSS feed (most
website builders have one built in, such as The Events Calendar for
WordPress, or Squarespace's events pages) lets us pick up new events on our
own, once a day. Reply with its link and we'll take it from there.

**If you post only on social media:** you can send each event through our
form at <https://479.guide/submit>. It asks for the name, date, place, and a
link to your post. We use your email only to ask about the event and delete
it 30 days after we've reviewed it.

What our reader fetches, how often, and how to ask us to stop are at
<https://479.guide/bot>.

Thanks,
[Your name]
479 Guide · bot@479.guide
